Norming debrief

Ten minutes, whole group. The point is the calibration, not the score.

  • Which indicator split the circle most? What made us read it differently?
  • What did a Yes actually look like in the veteran's room? Say it in one concrete sentence.
  • Where did we grade a new teacher's effort instead of the bar? Name it plainly.
  • What is our shared definition of a Yes on that indicator, going forward?
